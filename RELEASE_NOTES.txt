Parleq 0.25.0

A release focused on never losing a dictation — recover one you lost, plus
fixes that stop dictations from being lost in the first place — alongside
hands-free recording, in-place editing, configurable gestures, a gentler first
run, and a rebuilt on-device model downloader.

### Added

- **Recover your last dictation.** If a dictation gets lost — you pressed Esc
  by mistake, double-tapped Enter too fast, or focus jumped to the wrong app —
  bring it back: hold your dictation hotkey and tap R, or choose "Recover last
  dictation" from the menu bar. Parleq re-runs your most recent recording
  through cleanup and drops it back into the review overlay. The audio stays in
  memory only, is never written to disk, and is cleared when you quit.
- **Hands-free (continuous) recording.** Double-tap your hotkey and release to
  keep recording without holding the key down; tap once to stop.
- **Edit the transcript in place.** Press E in the review overlay to fix a word
  or two directly instead of re-dictating. ⌘↵ accepts, Esc discards.
- **Configurable hotkey gestures.** Settings → Hotkey lets you choose what a
  double-tap-and-release does (continuous recording, quick mode, plain
  dictation, or nothing).
- **A live "not hearing your mic" warning** appears if your microphone stops
  delivering audio mid-dictation.

### Changed

- **A hasty Enter during cleanup now waits for the full result** instead of
  pasting a half-finished fragment and cancelling the rest. A brief "Finishing…"
  cue shows while it completes. (Enter on the raw transcript before cleanup
  starts still accepts the raw text as-is; Esc still cancels.)
- **A smoother first run.** The Accessibility permission is now introduced in
  context by the setup wizard rather than prompted at launch, and the app stays
  running and usable while you grant it.
- **Rebuilt on-device model download** with real, byte-accurate progress and
  resume-after-interruption (even after a force-quit).

### Fixed

- **Dictations no longer come back silent when another app changes your audio
  setup.** A multi-channel input (e.g. forced by a video call) could make
  Parleq capture pure silence and discard the whole dictation. Capture now
  handles multi-channel input correctly and rebuilds itself if your device or
  format changes mid-dictation, keeping what you spoke after the change.
- **A dead microphone is now reported**, not silently hidden — Parleq shows
  "Didn't catch any audio — check your microphone" so you know to retry.
- **The keyboard can no longer get stuck** if you revoke Accessibility access
  while Parleq is running.
- **Fast double-taps are recognized again** (a 0.19.0 timing floor could reject
  quick double-taps, especially with Karabiner-Elements).
