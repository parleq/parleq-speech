Parleq 0.28.0

More precise dictionary biasing — everyday words are far less likely to be
wrongly replaced by a similar-sounding dictionary term.

### Changed

- Dictionary biasing over-corrects less. On-device speech recognition is now
  more conservative about swapping a spoken word for an acoustically similar
  dictionary term, so common words ("cloud", "item", and the like) are much
  less likely to be turned into one of your dictionary entries. Tuned against a
  corpus of real recordings — it cuts these over-corrections by roughly 3× with
  little effect on genuine recoveries.

- "Parleq" recognized from one more mishearing. The built-in "Parleq"
  dictionary term picks up an additional common way speech recognition mishears
  the app's name.
