Parleq 0.43.0

Voiceprints that carry over on their own, and tighter enterprise controls.

Enrolled terms carry over across speech-model updates. When a speech-model change means your voiceprints have to be rebuilt, Parleq now re-derives them from your stored enrollment audio — including acronyms and initialisms the model spells out differently than before (an enrolled "E2E" heard as "E to E"), which previously had to be re-enrolled by hand. And if a term genuinely can't be carried over, the menu bar now shows a persistent "a voice term needs re-enrolling" prompt so you find out without opening Settings.

Tighter enterprise controls. IT admins can pin the refinement mode (Raw, on-device Instant, or cloud Polished) with a new managed-configuration key. Pinning cleanup to "none" for a no-cloud lockdown now also forces refinement off the cloud — closing a gap where a follow-up voice command could still reach a cloud provider after the 0.40.0 change made cleanup and refinement independent.
