Parleq 0.25.2

A follow-up hotfix: 0.25.1 reverted the speech engine to the wrong version
and didn't fully fix the dictionary regression.

### Fixed

- **Custom dictionary over-insertion is now actually fixed.** 0.25.1 reverted
  the on-device speech engine to FluidAudio 0.14.8, believing the
  over-aggressive dictionary biasing was introduced in 0.15.x. It wasn't — the
  regression was introduced in FluidAudio 0.14.8 itself (an upstream rework of
  the vocabulary-rescoring pipeline, tuned only against long, distinctive
  terms), so reverting to 0.14.8 still over-fired (ordinary words replaced by
  your dictionary terms, even with cleanup turned off). Pinned the engine to
  exactly 0.14.5, the version 0.24.1 shipped, so transcription and dictionary
  biasing behave as they did then. The 0.25.1 pin also used a version range
  that silently resolved to the newest 0.14.x; the engine is now pinned to an
  exact version.
