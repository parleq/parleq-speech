Parleq 0.41.0

Voiceprints that learn from your corrections.

If you've enrolled a voiceprint for a name or term and it occasionally fires on a similar-sounding everyday word, Parleq now learns from your fix. Correct it once — by undoing the correction or editing the word back by hand — and Parleq refines that term's voiceprint from the corrected word's sound, so the same mistake stops recurring. No re-enrollment needed.

It's fully on-device and private: only an encrypted voice embedding is stored (never audio), it never leaves your Mac, and it's deleted along with the voiceprint. The feature is on by default for enrolled terms, with a "Refine voiceprints from corrections" switch in Settings → Dictionary, and undoing a wrong refinement backs it out automatically.
