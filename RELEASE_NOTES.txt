Parleq 0.45.0

Voiceprints now work in every cleanup mode.

A voiceprint keeps a dictionary term from replacing an ordinary word that sounds like it. Until now that check only ran in on-device Instant cleanup, so apps set to Polished could still swap the word. Parleq now applies your voiceprints before Polished cleanup too, including spoken refines. Dictations without your enrolled terms are unaffected.
